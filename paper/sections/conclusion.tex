\section{Conclusion}

We presented a protocol and artifact for federated elections in which local
tallies remain encrypted, accepted submissions are proof-checked and signed,
the aggregate is maintained on-chain, and only the final global tally is
opened. The construction is intentionally narrower than a full remote voting
system: local vote secrecy and local counting remain outside the protocol
boundary. Within that boundary, the artifact shows that encrypted local
tallies, transcript inclusion, homomorphic aggregation, and proof-checked
threshold opening can be combined in a reproducible on-chain workflow.
